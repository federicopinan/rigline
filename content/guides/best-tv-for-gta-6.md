GTA 6 is a night-driving, neon, story game as much as it is a shooter. The screen that helps is the one with contrast and size, not the one with the highest refresh rate sticker. This guide uses the [sample catalog](/products) only. The prices below are samples.

## What actually matters at launch

The game launches on PS5 and Xbox Series. Both can output 4K, and a game of this size is going to spend most of its time well under 120 frames. A 120 Hz set is enough headroom. A 240 Hz monitor does not unlock a mode the console does not have.

Size is the real split:

- A 55-inch TV is a couch setup. You sit back, the city fills the room, and a desk is the wrong place for it.
- A 42-inch OLED can live on a deep desk. It is still a TV.
- A 27-inch monitor is the competitive desk. It is sharper up close and worse at "I am in the car."

HDR and black level matter more here than they do in a bright esports game. That is why the cinematic weight in the builder gives the screen about 58% of whatever is left after the console.

## The sample picks

**[TCL QM7 55-inch](/products/tcl-qm7-55).** Lowest sample offer is $529 at Walmart in the seed. Mini-LED, 55 inches, 120 Hz. This is the screen the builder actually chooses for a [$1,500 cinematic PS5 setup](/build?budget=1500&platform=ps5&play=cinematic&currency=USD): disc PS5 at a $499 sample, this TV, a headset, a DualSense, and a 1 TB SSD.

**[LG C4 42-inch OLED](/products/lg-c4-42).** Lowest sample offer is $979. OLED is the contrast pick for night driving. It does not fit a $1,500 screen slot once the builder has reserved money for audio and storage. It does fit a [$2,500 cinematic PS5 setup](/build?budget=2500&platform=ps5&play=cinematic&currency=USD).

**[Sony Bravia X90L 55-inch](/products/sony-bravia-x90l-55).** Sample prices sit between the TCL and the OLED, around $679 to $748. It loses to the TCL on price in the $1,500 slot and to the C4 on quality once the budget reaches the OLED. It is in the catalog so the comparison is visible, not because it wins a default tier.

**[Samsung Odyssey G6 27-inch](/products/samsung-odyssey-g6-27).** Lowest sample offer is $319. This is what a [$1,000 balanced PS5 build](/build?budget=1000&platform=ps5&play=balanced&currency=USD) buys, because a $529 TV does not fit after a $499 console. That is the builder telling the truth: under $1,000 you get a monitor, not a living-room TV.

## How to use this

If you want the TV, start at $1,500 cinematic and open the result. If the screen line is still a monitor, the allocation could not hold a panel. Raising the budget is the fix. Marking a console as already owned does the same thing, because the whole budget then moves to the screen.
