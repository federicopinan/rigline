A GTA 6 session is long. The headset has to stay comfortable and the mic has to work for a party chat. It does not have to be a studio pair. This page only counts sets in the [sample catalog](/products) whose lowest in-stock sample offer is under $100.

## Under $100 in the seed

**[Turtle Beach Stealth 600 Gen 3](/products/turtle-beach-stealth-600).** Lowest sample offer is $69 at Walmart. Wireless, tagged for PS5, Xbox, and PC. This is the set a [$600 competitive Xbox build](/build?budget=600&platform=xbox&play=competitive&currency=USD) and an [$800 competitive PS5 build](/build?budget=800&platform=ps5&play=competitive&currency=USD) both land on. The slot is small after the console, and $69 fits.

**[HyperX Cloud III](/products/hyperx-cloud-iii).** Lowest in-stock sample offer is $99 at Amazon and Best Buy. The Walmart row in the seed is $89 and marked out of stock, so it does not win the badge. Wired, works on both consoles, and it is the pick on a [$1,000 balanced PS5 build](/build?budget=1000&platform=ps5&play=balanced&currency=USD) because the headset allocation there can hold $99 and the quality score is higher than the Stealth.

If you only want one under $100 and you sit on the couch, take the Stealth. If you do not mind a cable and you want the better sample score, take the Cloud III.

## Just over the line

**[PlayStation Pulse Elite](/products/sony-pulse-elite).** Lowest sample is $139. It is a PS5 headset, not an Xbox one. It loses the under-$100 cut on price. It is the right next step when you already own the console and the screen and the headset slot is what is left.

**[SteelSeries Arctis Nova 7](/products/steelseries-arctis-nova-7).** Lowest sample is $159 at Target. This is the long-session default once the budget is $1,500 cinematic. It works on both consoles, which matters if you have not locked a platform.

**[Sony INZONE H9](/products/sony-inzone-h9).** Lowest sample is $189. Cinematic bias, PS5 and PC only. The builder picks it at $1,800 and $2,500 cinematic because the headset weight finally has room. It is not an under-$100 answer.

## What not to optimize

Spatial audio marketing will not fix a screen that is too small, and a $200 headset on a $600 budget means you skipped the console or the display. The builder refuses that trade. Run your number in the [builder](/build) and read the headset reasons. If the slot is empty, the ceiling was below every in-stock set.
